# Trust, Search, AI Discovery, and Reputation

Status: **P0 PUBLIC TRUTH / P1 DISCOVERY EXPERIENCE**

## Canonical truth

`trust_facts`, `trust_documents`, `trust_versions` and `trust_sources` are the
single governed fact layer for public pages, metadata, structured data,
`llms.txt`, sitemap, support and PUTDUK AI retrieval.

Every fact has an owner, visibility, effective version, source and updated-at
timestamp. Contradictory published facts block publication.

Public content states plainly:

- PUTDUK is a virtual mining platform;
- it is not actual blockchain mining;
- rewards, difficulty and economy rules are internal PUTDUK rules;
- PUTDUK START conversion is capped at KRW 5,000 and does not require funding
  before its eligible withdrawal;
- operational availability and policies are versioned and may be paused safely.

## Discoverability

Use semantic HTML, reviewed titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, `ko-KR`,
future `ja-JP`/hreflang readiness, sitemap, robots and appropriate
Organization/WebSite/SoftwareApplication/Breadcrumb JSON-LD. Public About, How
It Works, Facts, Verification, FAQ, Status, Changelog, trial, mining/economy,
deposit and withdrawal guides expose version/freshness metadata.

Admin, account, wallet, KYC and private member routes are never indexed.

## Integrity

Never fabricate reviews, members, active-user counts, testimonials, media
coverage or outside AI answers. Brand monitoring terms such as `퍼뜩`,
`PUTDUK MINING`, `퍼뜩 출금` and `퍼뜩 사기` are used only to find unanswered
factual questions and inconsistencies in official content.

Growth AI may draft factual updates and acquisition variants. Launching paid
media or spending budget always requires operator approval. Acquisition
attribution follows the factual lifecycle through welcome withdrawal, funding,
real mining and 7/30-day activity without coupling ads to search/AI answers.
